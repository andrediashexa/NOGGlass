# Project Authors

## TL;DR

NOGGlass was **idealized and created by Marcelo Gondim da Cunha**, who set out to give the community a free, quality Looking Glass and invited **André Dias** to build it with him. It is designed, engineered, and maintained by both as an open-source, production-grade network diagnostic platform for Autonomous Systems and telecommunications operators.
