# Code of Conduct

## TL;DR

Be respectful, assume good intentions, and focus on ideas rather than people. Technical disagreements and constructive criticism are welcome; personal attacks are never tolerated.

---

## Our Principles

- **Be respectful:** Treat fellow contributors, operators, and users with professional courtesy.
- **Assume good intentions:** Approach discussions with the assumption that everyone wants to improve the project.
- **Discuss ideas instead of people:** Keep debates centered on technical architecture, protocols, and code quality.
- **Technical disagreements are welcome:** Rigorous debate leads to better engineering decisions and safer network operations.
- **Personal attacks are not tolerated:** Demeaning, harassing, or hostile behavior will result in exclusion from the project.
- **Constructive criticism is encouraged:** Offer actionable feedback and evidence-based rationale.
- **Shared objective:** Everyone is here to build more reliable, secure, and performant telecommunications software.
